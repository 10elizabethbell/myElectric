# MYElectric — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (GitHub Pages), Cal Sans from Google Fonts, no frameworks, no tracking
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (a one-page site for a local electrician)

## Users
- **Homeowners in South Jersey** who need an electrician: new lighting, fixtures, upgrades, repairs. They find the link on Facebook, on a phone, often inside Facebook's in-app browser. (Inferred from the 856 area code and the flyer's "Light Up Your Home".)
- **The owner of MYElectric**, who receives the site for free. It has to look like their real business site on first sight.
- Contractors in NJ trade groups also see the flyers (the brief found them in five contractor groups); the site serves homeowners first. (Inferred.)

## Purpose
Turn a homeowner's "I need an electrician" into a call or a text to 856-628-2241. Replace the current myelectricllc.com shell (headline, email signup, Name/Email form; no services, no phone).

## Positioning
A licensed and insured electrician who does clean, modern, custom work. Source: their own flyer ("CLEAN MODERN CUSTOM DESIGNS", "LICENSED & INSURED", "Light Up Your Home").

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact by phone (856-628-2241, printed on their flyer). Whether it takes texts is unknown, so calling stays one tap away everywhere a text button appears.
- Email info@myelectricllc.com appears only in the footer.
- No towns, hours, owner name, prices, reviews or work photos are known. Say nothing about them.
- The flyer prints "LIC 19291": unverified. Do not show a license number.
- Name is MYELECTRIC / MYElectric. Do not add "LLC" to the name.
- Facebook: only a group post is known (no business page URL).

## Brand commitments
- Wordmark "MYELECTRIC" (no logo file obtained; flyer images need a Facebook login).
- No brand colours known (existing site is a grey default template). Palette chosen for the trade: navy and a warm amber accent. (Inferred; replace with the flyer's colours once seen.)
- Keep it neutral and service-focused; don't reuse the breast-cancer-awareness promo artwork.

## Evidence on hand
- Flyer claims: "LICENSED & INSURED", "CLEAN MODERN CUSTOM DESIGNS", "Light Up Your Home".
- Phone and email from the flyer.
- No photos of real jobs. The hero image is generated (an example of finished lighting, not their job).

## Principles
- Phone first. The first phone screen shows who, what, where (South Jersey) and how to reach them.
- Only facts from the brief; gaps go to HANDOFF.md, not onto the page.
- Speak as the business ("we", since the brief doesn't say one person or a crew — inferred default).
